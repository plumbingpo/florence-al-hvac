Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Huntsville

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected through a full season, can take out the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
